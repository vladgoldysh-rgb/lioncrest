# How We Build Venture Capital Websites
*Author: The Pent Team (Pent Studio, October 8, 2023)*

To create something truly exceptional, one must possess a genuine passion for it and gain a profound understanding of the clients' requirements. This perspective is crucial in web design for Venture Capital companies.

## Step 1 - Identifying Brand Messaging & Target Audience
Venture capital firms typically have two primary target audiences: startups seeking funding and potential Limited Partners (LPs). 

A common thread among these users is their interest in the venture capitalist's past and current investments. Therefore, a **robust portfolio section or page** becomes a central focus for a venture capital website. It should offer advanced sorting and filtering options:
- **Industries** (tech, space, health, etc.)
- **Listed or not** (If the company is public or private)
- **Exited or active investment** (Are you still a shareholder)
- **Region** (Where is the company located)

## Step 2 - Investing strategy and philosophy
Now that users can view your past investments, it's essential to communicate *why* you made these investments and what sets you apart from other VCs. 
- Introduce a concise overview of the investment strategy on the landing page (e.g., three main components of the strategy).
- Include Call-To-Actions (CTAs) that guide users to delve deeper into the firm's philosophy.

## Step 3 - The Team is a Two Way Street
The team at the Venture Capital company is crucial when startups (and LPs) are selecting a partner.
- **Comprehensive team page:** Offers a clear understanding of the individuals behind the fund, their specific roles, and past accomplishments.
- **LP Confidence:** Introducing the team ensures that LPs have confidence in the hands they are entrusting their investments to.
- **Contact Info:** Provide direct contact information for team members to avoid the frustration of generic 'info@' emails.

## Step 4 - Cherry on top features
These features can elevate the website's completeness:
- **Pitch Deck Upload Feature:** An area where startups can easily submit their pitch decks, streamlining the evaluation process.
- **Application Procedure:** An overview of the application process, including a typical timeline for investment evaluation.
- **News and Press Updates:** A newsroom with updates on portfolio companies and internal happenings.
- **Social Proof:** Testimonials from previous partnerships that highlight your excellence as a collaborative partner.
- **Investor Access:** A dedicated login feature for LPs to access customized information regarding returns and related data.
