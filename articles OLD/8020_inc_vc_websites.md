# Websites built for what’s next in Venture Capital (8020.inc)
*Source: 8020.inc Website Copy*

8020 is a Webflow design agency that partners with VC firms to design websites that build credibility with LPs, highlight portfolio companies, and communicate value to founders.

## Key Insights & Approach to VC Websites

**1. The Dual Purpose of a VC Website**
- **Prove you're the ones to do it:** A VC's job is to spot what’s next. The website's job is to prove they can do it.
- **Build credibility with LPs:** A platform that builds trust with investors and limited partners.
- **Communicate value to founders:** Messaging that resonates with founders and attracts the right deals.

**2. Discovery & Messaging**
- Clarify how you think, invest, and partner. 
- Turn abstract ideas into a compelling narrative to create alignment with founders, LPs, and the broader ecosystem.

**3. Design for Credibility**
- Credibility is built through **clarity and restraint**. 
- Design refined layouts that showcase the portfolio, case studies, and trust signals *without feeling promotional*. 
- The result should be a confident presence that reflects how you operate behind the scenes.

**4. Scalability & Integrations**
- A VC website should support more than marketing. It should integrate CRMs, deal flow tools, investor dashboards, and internal systems into a seamless experience. 
- It becomes a functional extension of the firm’s operations.

**5. Ongoing Evolution**
- The portfolio is always changing, and the website should reflect that. It needs clean systems and reusable components so the site evolves as funds, teams, and portfolios grow.
