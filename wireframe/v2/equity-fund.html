Lioncrest Venture Fund

Investing in Capital Efficient Companies with Product Market Fit

Initial Ticket

$500K – $3MM, with reserves for follow-on allocations. Active board partnership model.

Target Stage

Seed+ and Series A platforms, typically with revenues >$1.5MM ARR.

Geography

North America and Israel. Support structures for scaling global teams in the US market.

Investment Fit

Excluding hardware, CPG, and FDA-regulated medical devices. We only invest where we add value.

While Lioncrest invests across a broad spectrum of B2B technologies, we see exceptional opportunity in AI-driven, vertically focused companies addressing mission-critical pain points in highly regulated industries.

Venture Fund FAQ

What stage do you invest at?
Seed+ through Series A, typically with more than $1.5MM in ARR and a clear path to capital efficiency.
Do you take board seats?
Yes — we take board seats on most of our investments and stay actively involved. We are hands-on operator partners, not passive check-writers.
What if my company is outside your listed sectors?
We are primarily focused on cybersecurity, fintech, digital health, supply chain/logistics, defense/gov tech, and legaltech — but if there is a strong case for fit and we can add value, we will review the deal.
How does the credit fund differ from the equity fund?
Venture equity is about backing breakout upside. Prospeq Credit is structured financing for cash-flow positive companies with proven revenue, built around risk-adjusted, yield-generating debt structures.
