Change Log (v2.0)

[NEW PAGE] Founder Application Hub

Created a centralized hub for all founder inquiries. In the old wireframe, forms were scattered across the fund pages, making it hard to manage. Now, they are housed on one page with a simple tab switch.

Fund Specific Criteria Selection

Divided the applications into two distinct tabs: * **Equity Fund:** Asks for "Current ARR" (Seed+/Series A qualification) and specific vertical sectors (Cyber, Fintech, etc.). * **Credit Fund (Prospeq):** Asks for "Annual Revenue" categories ($2M-$5M, $5M-$10M, $10M+) to match debt underwriting standards.

Context-Aware Pre-selection

The tabs are driven by a simple URL query parameter (e.g. `?fund=credit`), automatically opening the correct fund form when a founder navigates here from the corresponding fund detail page.

Cross-Link Disclaimer

Added a clear link directing LPs to their relations hub to prevent qualification pollution.
