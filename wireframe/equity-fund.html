How We Invest

Partners

Investing is a long-term partnership. We're a small team that works closely with our companies through the hard stretches, not just the headline moments. We value the team as much as the product and the market.

Investment Focus

We back founders who've found a structural advantage - often regulatory - and are building the software to defend it. We only invest where we believe we can genuinely move the company forward.

Sector Focus

Lioncrest invests across B2B technology, with particular focus on AI-driven, vertically-focused companies solving mission-critical problems in highly regulated industries. Verticals we pay special attention to include:

FINTECH/INSURTECH DEFENSE/GOV TECH DIGITAL HEALTH SUPPLY CHAIN & LOGISTICS AVIATION/AIRSPACE LEGALTECH

How We Value-Add

We provide institutional support systems designed to scale B2B software companies beyond initial product-market fit.

GTM & Outreach Playbooks

We help founders transition from relationship-based sales to repeatable, metrics-driven outbound process engines.

US & Israel Corridors

We assist Israel-based engineering teams in scaling corporate business development and marketing operations in North America.

Recruiting & Networks

We plug early-stage companies directly into seasoned B2B operator networks to build professional executive boards.
