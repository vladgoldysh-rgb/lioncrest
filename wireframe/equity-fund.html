VENTURE CAPITAL

Lioncrest Venture Fund

Investing in Capital Efficient Companies with Product Market Fit

Initial Investment Size

$500K – $3MM, with a portion of our fund reserved for follow-on. We play an active role in all of our investments and have board seats on most of them.

Stage

Seed+ and Series A, typically with revenues of >$1.5MM ARR

Geography

North America and Israel. We invest in companies that view the U.S. as a core target market and actively support their growth and expansion in the region.

Fit

Certain characteristics fall outside our investment focus — hardware, medical devices requiring FDA approval, consumer packaged goods, and companies targeting non-U.S. markets. If we can't add meaningful value, we don't invest.

While Lioncrest invests across a broad spectrum of B2B technologies, we see exceptional opportunity in AI-driven, vertically focused companies addressing mission-critical pain points in highly regulated industries — where compliance, security, and data integrity are not optional, but core to business.
