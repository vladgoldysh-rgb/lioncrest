Change Log (v2.0)

[NEW PAGE] LP Contact Hub

Created a dedicated, standalone routing page for LPs. In the old wireframe, LPs were forced to use the same generic forms as founders, providing no clear path for booking introductory calls or sharing institutional due diligence details.

Dual Tabbed Layout

Includes a "Book a Meeting" tab containing a Calendly embed and a "Send a Message" tab with a short contact form. LPs can choose their preferred engagement route immediately.

LP-Specific Qualifications

The message form fields are custom-tailored to ask for "Firm/Organization Name" and "Type of Investor" to filter out non-LP leads early.

Cross-Link Disclaimer

Added a clear disclaimer at the bottom directing founders to their own application hub, removing any potential confusion.
