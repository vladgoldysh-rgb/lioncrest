Insights & Notes

Page Note

Two-stage interaction pattern, directly modeled on Lux Capital's portfolio page. Stage 1 is a quick-preview modal (opens on top of the grid, doesn't navigate away), Stage 2 is a full company profile page (separate URL, reached via "View Company Profile" inside the modal). Faster to build and matches exactly how Lux Capital — one of the reference brands you named — does it.

Interaction Guide — Company Preview Modal

Clicking on any company card in the grid opens a quick-preview modal overlay with a dimmed background (Stage 1). You do not navigate to a new page. Prev/next navigation lives inside the modal so a visitor can browse company to company without closing it — exactly like the Lux Capital pattern.

Section 2 — Get In Touch

Someone browsing the Portfolio page is likely either an LP doing diligence or a founder scoping out who else you've backed — either way, they might want to reach out right from here instead of hunting for a contact page. Same short-form pattern as the rest of the site.
