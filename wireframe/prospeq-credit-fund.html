Page Note

Same template as the Equity Fund page, for consistency — page hierarchy, sections, and flow are identical across both fund pages, only the content differs. Keeping the structure identical so nothing reads as "the credit fund got the lesser treatment," regardless of which page you look at first.

Section 4 — Trust Signal (Testimonials)

Built as a case-study format with a tab switcher — because a real case shows how the value happened, not just that someone was happy. The 5 real Prospeq testimonials are still used elsewhere (Home page, "Client & Founder Trust" section). The Equity fund page uses the same case-study format for the same reason, so credit doesn't read as the lighter-treated fund next to it.

Filtered view of the shared Portfolio component, filtered to Prospeq-funded companies. Note to self: the current Prospeq portfolio page lists 20+ founder names with no full company descriptions — needs reconciling/simplifying before real content goes in. Not wireframing all 20+, just a representative sample of 4.

FAQ

What does Prospeq mean?
Prospeq is Lioncrest's private credit fund — built around the idea of prospective growth financing for technology companies that are already generating revenue.
What types of companies does Prospeq invest in?
Technology companies across B2B, B2C, tech-enabled, marketplace, SaaS, and hardware business models — typically generating $2MM–$10MM in annual revenue.
Does Prospeq invest in certain sectors?
We're broad rather than narrow — B2B, B2C, tech-enabled, marketplace, SaaS, and hardware are all in scope, as long as the fundamentals hold up.
How does the credit fund differ from the equity fund?
Prospeq is structured financing for companies with proven revenue, built around risk-adjusted, cash-flow-driven returns — not equity upside. Equity is about backing companies still building toward that outsized growth.
