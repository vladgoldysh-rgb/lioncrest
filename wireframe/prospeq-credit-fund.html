Fund Overview

$500K – $2MM. Structured debt vehicles designed for operational scale.

Stage

Geography

North America technology hubs. Structured to align with regional operating codes.

Investment Fit

Prospeq takes a broader view than our equity fund — we're not chasing the next category-defining breakout, we're backing technology companies with real revenue and a clear path to repay. Risk-adjusted, cash-flow-driven financing across a wide range of business models.

How We Invest

Non-Dilutive Growth

We provide runway for scaling companies without sacrificing ownership. Our debt facilities are structured to optimize your balance sheet during critical growth phases, bridging the gap between equity rounds or paving the way to profitability.

Speed & Structure

We underwrite against proven revenue and cash flow, allowing us to move quickly and provide flexible covenants that align with your operational reality rather than rigid banking requirements.

How We Value-Add

We provide institutional support systems designed to scale B2B software companies beyond initial product-market fit.

Balance Sheet Optimization

We help founders balance equity-dilutive runway extensions with lower-cost structured credit instruments.

Debt Underwriting Playbooks

We guide management teams in organizing operational cash-flows and metrics to prepare for institutional tier-1 bank debt.

Scale without Dilution

We assist cash-flow positive platforms in utilizing structured debt to acquire competitors or fund working capital runs.

Prospeq Credit FAQ

What does Prospeq mean?
Prospeq is Lioncrest's private credit fund — built around the idea of prospective growth financing for technology companies that are already generating revenue.
What types of companies does Prospeq invest in?
Technology companies across B2B, B2C, tech-enabled, marketplace, SaaS, and hardware business models — typically generating $2MM–$10MM in annual revenue.
Does Prospeq invest in certain sectors?
We're broad rather than narrow — B2B, B2C, tech-enabled, marketplace, SaaS, and hardware are all in scope, as long as the fundamentals hold up.
How does the credit fund differ from the equity fund?
Prospeq is structured financing for companies with proven revenue, built around risk-adjusted, cash-flow-driven returns — not equity upside. Equity is about backing companies still building toward that outsized growth.
