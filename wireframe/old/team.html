Insights & Notes

Page Note

Modeled on Viola's Team page — one shared team page with a fund-filter, not separate team pages per fund. Viola doesn't split Portfolio or Team into separate top-level nav destinations per fund — filtering happens within one shared page. Following the same principle here — keeps navigation simple while still letting a visitor narrow down by fund if they want to.

Section 2 — Get In Touch

Individual team members have their own "Contact Me" mailto on their profile pages, but someone might land on this page first without knowing which specific person to reach — so this gives them a general way in. Same short-form pattern as every other contact touchpoint on the site: minimal fields, prominent email beside it.
