Insights & Notes

Page Note

Uses the same shared fund-page template as the Prospeq — Credit Fund page, so both fund pages follow identical structure and are easier to maintain going forward. This mirrors Viola's own pattern — Viola Ventures, Viola Growth, Viola Credit, and Viola Fintech all follow the exact same page template (hero stats, "we help our companies with," domains, portfolio, team), just with different content per fund. Not something invented — borrowing a pattern from a fund already referenced. This page is also founder-facing — trust signals sit close to the contact form.

Section 2+3 — Sectors + The Team Behind This Fund

Team cards sit stacked below the sectors description, so it reads as description first, then team, not two competing columns. All three team members tagged to this fund on the Team page: Leib, Nitsan Nairdorf, and Zeba Rooheen.

Section 4 — Trust Signal (Case Study Switcher)

Built as a case-study format — before/after narrative, tab switcher between companies. A full case study works better than a flat trust badge because it's a piece of storytelling that shows how the value happened, not just that someone was happy. Using ANRA Technologies and Lassen Peak here since they're real portfolio companies with known, described products. The Before/After bullets are drafted from what's publicly described about each company's product; the quote and the result metric are real placeholders, not filled-in guesses — need the actual sourced material from you before this goes live.

Section 5 — Portfolio (subset)

Filtered view of the shared Portfolio component, same underlying data, just filtered to LC Equity companies. Not a separate portfolio system.

Section 6 — Contact

Kept this short, but the point isn't minimalism for its own sake — the form exists to validate fit fast. Company Name, Current ARR, and Sector map directly to your stated criteria. Note: the Prospeq contact form asks for Annual Revenue instead of Current ARR, since that's the relevant qualifying number for a credit deal — same idea, different metric.

Section 7 — FAQ

Same reasoning as the master FAQ on Our Approach — a good FAQ reveals more about the product than a bullet list would, and answers the objection before the contact form. This is the subset an equity-side founder would actually ask, plus one equity-specific question not on the master list, since sector fit is the most likely disqualifying question for someone reading this page.

VENTURE CAPITAL

Lioncrest Venture Fund

Investing in Capital Efficient Companies with Product Market Fit

Initial Investment Size

$500K – $3MM, with a portion of our fund reserved for follow-on. We play an active role in all of our investments and have board seats on most of them.

Stage

Seed+ and Series A, typically with revenues of >$1.5MM ARR

Geography

North America and Israel. We invest in companies that view the U.S. as a core target market and actively support their growth and expansion in the region.

Fit

Certain characteristics fall outside our investment focus — hardware, medical devices requiring FDA approval, consumer packaged goods, and companies targeting non-U.S. markets. If we can't add meaningful value, we don't invest.

While Lioncrest invests across a broad spectrum of B2B technologies, we see exceptional opportunity in AI-driven, vertically focused companies addressing mission-critical pain points in highly regulated industries — where compliance, security, and data integrity are not optional, but core to business.

FAQ

What stage do you invest at?
Seed+ through Series A, typically with more than $1.5MM in ARR.
Do you take board seats?
Yes — we take board seats on most of our investments and stay actively involved, not just check-writers.
What if my company is outside your listed sectors?
We're primarily focused on cybersecurity, fintech, digital health, supply chain/logistics, defense/gov tech, and legaltech — but if there's a strong case for fit, we're happy to hear it.
How does the credit fund differ from the equity fund?
Equity is about backing the upside — companies building toward outsized growth. Prospeq is structured financing for companies with proven revenue, built around risk-adjusted, cash-flow-driven returns.
