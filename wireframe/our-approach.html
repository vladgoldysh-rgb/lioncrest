What We Are

  • A dual-strategy platform — equity and credit, under one roof
  • Active board partners, not passive check-writers
  • Focused on regulated, mission-critical industries
  • Disciplined about fit — we only invest where we can add real value
  • Collaborators with the firms you'd compare us to — many are co-investors, not competitors

What We Are Not

  • A generalist, spray-and-pray fund
  • Investors in hardware, FDA-regulated medical devices, or consumer packaged goods
  • Focused on companies outside our core U.S. / Israel / North America market
  • A fund that takes a check and steps back

FAQ

What does Prospeq mean?
Prospeq is Lioncrest's private credit fund — built around the idea of prospective growth financing for technology companies that are already generating revenue.
What types of companies does Prospeq invest in?
Technology companies across B2B, B2C, tech-enabled, marketplace, SaaS, and hardware business models — typically generating $2MM–$10MM in annual revenue.
Does Prospeq invest in certain sectors?
We're broad rather than narrow — B2B, B2C, tech-enabled, marketplace, SaaS, and hardware are all in scope, as long as the fundamentals hold up.
What stage do you invest at?
On the equity side, Seed+ through Series A, typically with more than $1.5MM in ARR. On the credit side, companies with $2MM–$10MM in annual revenue.
Do you take board seats?
On the equity side, yes — we take board seats on most of our investments and stay actively involved. Credit investments are structured differently and don't carry the same board involvement.
How does the credit fund differ from the equity fund?
Equity is about backing the upside — companies building toward outsized growth. Prospeq is structured financing for companies with proven revenue, built around risk-adjusted, cash-flow-driven returns rather than equity upside.
