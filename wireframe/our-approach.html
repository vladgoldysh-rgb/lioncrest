Insights & Notes

Page Note

Structure is adapted from Greylock's "Edge" program page — numbered steps, "What We Are / What We Are Not," FAQ accordion. You don't have an accelerator program, so I'm not copying Edge's content, just its shape, filled with your actual investment philosophy from what you said on the call, Leib. Also keeping two pieces of your existing brand copy on purpose — the "It's In Our Name" Lion/Crest wordplay, and the contact@lioncrest.vc Get In Touch pattern — instead of dropping them for the new positioning. They don't conflict with LP-first messaging, and losing them would mean losing a genuine, already-approved piece of your voice.

Section 2 — Why Us

Kept word-for-word from your existing site. This has nothing to do with old vs. new positioning — it's your wordplay-based brand voice, so I'm leaving it exactly as-is.

Section 3 — How We Think

This is what gives the page structural "depth," the way Greylock's 4-step Edge process does — but built from what you actually said on the call, Leib, not a fictional program. Four steps, matching your own words: futuristic equity thesis, risk-adjusted credit discipline, regulated-industry focus, active/hands-on partnership through board seats.

Section 4 — What We Are / What We Are Not

Straight structural borrow from Greylock's Edge page — it's a proven format for sharp differentiation. Content is all Lioncrest-specific, pulled from your actual investment criteria (no hardware/medical device/non-US focus, from your existing "Fit" section) and from Leib's active-partner language on the call. Also added the "collaborators, not competitors" point here, Leib — you were clear on the call that firms like Aleph, Viola, and Greylock are potential co-investors, not competition. That's a real differentiator and it wasn't showing up anywhere on the actual site content until now.

Section 5 — FAQ

Format borrowed from Greylock's Edge FAQ accordion, but the questions come from your actual existing FAQ (previously on the Prospeq site) plus a few new ones relevant to the dual-fund structure. Built this way specifically so you see, Ryan, that your old FAQ content wasn't dropped — just relocated and expanded.

How We Think

01

Futuristic Thesis (Equity)

We invest in where the world is going, not where it already is — backing founders building categories that don't fully exist yet.

02

Risk-Adjusted Discipline (Credit)

Prospeq isn't about the exciting story. It's cash-flow-driven, structured financing for companies that are already proving their model.

03

Regulated-Industry Focus

We concentrate where compliance, security, and data integrity aren't optional — cybersecurity, fintech, digital health, legaltech, supply chain, defense/gov tech.

04

Active Partnership

We take board seats on most of our investments. We're not a firm that writes a check and disappears.

What We Are

  • A dual-strategy platform — equity and credit, under one roof
  • Active board partners, not passive check-writers
  • Focused on regulated, mission-critical industries
  • Disciplined about fit — we only invest where we can add real value
  • Collaborators with the firms you'd compare us to — many are co-investors, not competitors

What We Are Not

  • A generalist, spray-and-pray fund
  • Investors in hardware, FDA-regulated medical devices, or consumer packaged goods
  • Focused on companies outside our core U.S. / Israel / North America market
  • A fund that takes a check and steps back
  • Trying to "beat" other Tier-1 funds — we're often in the same deals together
