Change Log (v2.0)

Hero Action Integration

Introduced a single focused step for LPs ("See How We Invest") to eliminate CTA emptiness. Shifted the founder/partner photos to the same line as the copy to visually integrate them.

Proof of Life Insights

Featured recent thought leadership articles directly below the Hero (inspired by Aleph.vc). This validates the fund's activity level immediately to prospective LPs.

Scalable Platform Nomenclature

Removed the restrictive 'Two Funds' branding to support future product expansions. Replaced it with positioning emphasizing 'Capital Built Around Where You Are' and 'Different Capital for Different Moments'.

Centralized LP/Founder CTA

Replaced the fragmented forms at the bottom of the page with the universal CTA block. This routes traffic into LP or Founder queues cleanly.

Co-investor Signal Grid

Added a 'Co-investor Signal' block featuring logos of co-investment partners (Viola, Aleph, Greylock, Lux Capital) to establish trust for emerging managers.
