Insights & Notes

Section 1 — Header

Nav stays minimal — matches what you already have (Home / Portfolio / Team / Funds & Strategy / Investor Login). Investor Login isn't my idea, by the way — it's already on your site, and every reference fund we looked at (Viola, Lux Capital, Kickstart) has it too. Keeping it because it signals institutional seriousness.

Section 2 — Hero

Leib, you told me on the call you're "a little bit of a visual person" and want team credibility visible right away. So the Hero carries both of you together — one shared photo instead of two separate headshots, quote overlaid on the image, matching Aleph's homepage pattern. Reads more like a real partnership than two isolated portraits. Also covers the "team teaser" need without a dedicated section, since the rest of your team gets its own spot further down.

Section 3 — Scroll-Reveal Intro

Bridges the Hero and the Two-Fund Cards. Short line, words fade in as you scroll — same idea as that "boutique AI agency" animated-text example. Meant to hold attention right before the fund-selection moment. It's basically your own existing Funds & Strategy copy — reusing it since it's already clear and already yours.

Section 4 — Two-Fund Cards

Solves the exact thing you named on the call, Leib — "hard messaging the two different products to two different types of investors." Right after the Hero, not buried, so a visitor self-sorts fast. Loosely modeled on Viola's fund-grid, but 2 cards instead of 4, and each adds a "who leads this fund" line Viola's cards don't have.

Section 5 — Portfolio Carousel

Modeled on Greylock's homepage proof carousel. Drones/ANRA leads first specifically because that's the example you wanted, Leib — "these drones... I've got video and clips." Headline had to stay generic since 3-4 companies rotate through the same spot.

Section 6 — Client & Founder Trust

These testimonials already live on your Prospeq site — 5 quotes, revenue growth, partner support. Not new content, just migrated. Framing it as trust for the whole Lioncrest brand, not "Prospeq-only," since both funds sit under one site now.

Section 7 — Team Teaser

Hero already covers Leib and Ryan. This shows the team runs deeper — Zeba and Nitsan otherwise never show up on the homepage. For a 2-year-old, first fund, team depth is a real trust signal — shouldn't rest on two founder photos alone.

Section 8 — Footer

Email layout follows Viola Credit's "Connect With Us" — multiple purpose-specific addresses, prominent, direct mailto. Adds onto what you have now (one generic contact@). Flagging as a recommended upgrade — need to confirm with Ryan whether purpose-specific inboxes actually exist before locking addresses.

Section 2 — Hero

Requires a shared photo of Leib and Ryan. Quote needs to be a joint quote drafted and approved separately, attributed to "Leib Bolel & Ryan Edwards, Managing Partners".

Section 3 — Portfolio Media

Large format videos or photos needed for ANRA (drone technology), Lassen Peak, PAXAFE, and Novella.

Section 4 — Prospeq Testimonials

Pull 3 of the 5 existing Prospeq testimonials verbatim from prospeq.co once source text is confirmed; do not write any new testimonial copy.
