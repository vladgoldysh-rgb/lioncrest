PAXAFE

PAXAFE is an enterprise SaaS platform that utilizes machine learning and IoT sensor data to provide predictive, real-time visibility for cold chain shipping. Shippers in pharmaceutical, healthcare, and high-value food supply chains use PAXAFE to track temperature, humidity, and location anomalies, preventing product spoilage before it happens.

Unlike traditional passive loggers that only report failures after the cargo has arrived, PAXAFE's risk engine analyzes environmental trends to predict spoilage and route delays hours in advance. This enables operators to intervene, redirect shipments, and preserve critical inventory.

"Lioncrest invested in PAXAFE because it addresses a massive, highly regulated operational pain point in global pharmaceutical logistics. By transitioning supply chains from reactive tracking to predictive risk mitigation, PAXAFE provides a critical operational ROI for global enterprise shippers, representing exactly the capital-efficient, category-leading software we target." — Leib Bolel, Managing Partner
