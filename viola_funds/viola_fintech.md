Viola Fintech is a Venture Capital fund investing in Fintech, Insurtech, Regtech and Proptech startups across US, Canada, Europe and Israel.
Led by experienced operators with decades of combined experience in the Fintech space, we are passionate about partnering with entrepreneurs who are set out to reshape the future of financial services.
Backed by some of the most prominent financial institutions in the world, we facilitate their collaboration with innovative entrepreneurs to accelerate the transformation of financial services globally.
